## What affects the price of commercial laundry disposal?

Because a single stacked laundromat dryer and a row of bolted 60 kg hotel washers require completely different rigging, we base pricing on the physical job details rather than guesswork:

- **Machine weight and dimensions:** Heavy rigid-mount extractors need steel skates, extra crew members, and tail-lift capacity.

## Preparing your laundry room for smooth collection

A quick check before our truck arrives prevents site delays and keeps your facility compliant:

- **Confirm utility sign-off:** Verify with your MEP technician that power breakers are locked off, water supply valves are closed, and gas or steam lines are capped.
- **Empty drums and chemical lines:** Remove any remaining linen, detergent drums, and loose lint from traps so water or soap does not spill during tilting.
- **Measure tight doorways:** Let us know the width of the narrowest door frame or corridor turn along the route so we bring the right skates or panel-removal tools.
- **Reserve the loading bay:** If your building requires a security NOC or service-lift slot, share the gate-pass contact details when booking.

## Municipal bulky waste vs private commercial removal

Dubai Municipality's free bulky-waste service is designed for eligible residential household items only and does not collect commercial machinery, industrial plant-room equipment, or bolted laundry installations from businesses or development zones. If you are clearing a hotel, laundromat, or staff accommodation block, a private licensed crew is required to unbolt plinths, rig heavy units through service corridors, and haul scrap metal to approved recovery yards.
