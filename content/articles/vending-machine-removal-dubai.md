## Preparing your vending machine for collection

A few minutes of preparation before our crew arrives keeps the pickup fast and avoids delays at the loading bay:

- **Empty stock and coin mechanisms:** Remove all bottles, snacks, coffee beans, and cash cassettes, as loose items shift inside the cabinet during tilting.
- **Defrost and dry refrigerated units:** Unplug cold-drink and smart-fridge machines ahead of time and empty the condensation drip tray so water does not spill onto lobby carpets or lift floors.
- **Disconnect water-plumbed coffee towers:** Turn off the inline shut-off valve and disconnect braided water hoses before our team arrives.
- **Check doorway and lift clearances:** Measure narrow pantry doors or corridor turns and flag any tight spots when you send your WhatsApp photos.

## E-waste and metal recycling for old vending machines

A commercial vending machine is a mix of heavy sheet steel, copper wiring, aluminium cooling fins, tempered glass, and electronic payment and control boards. Simply dumping a machine in general waste wastes recoverable materials and risks environmental penalties.

Once collected, machines are transported to licensed processing facilities in Dubai where materials are separated responsibly. Steel cabinets and aluminium frames go to scrap metal recyclers, while printed circuit boards, card readers, and wiring harnesses are routed through proper [e-waste disposal in Dubai](/e-waste-disposal-dubai/) channels.
