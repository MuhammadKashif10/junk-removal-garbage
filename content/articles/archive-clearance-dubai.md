## Dismantling mobile compactors and floor rail systems

The core technical hurdle during an archive clearance is dismantling the rolling compactor mechanism without causing structural damage to the office sub-floor. Mobile compactors are installed using precision-levelled steel guidance rails anchored directly into the structural concrete floor slab or embedded flush within raised computer flooring.

Our technicians execute dismantling with engineering precision:

- **Shelf and carriage de-stacking:** Shelves are unhooked, cross-braces unbolted, and vertical uprights detached from the rolling chassis.
- **Drive mechanism de-coupling:** Handwheel cranks, chain-drive sprockets, and internal gearbox locks are stripped down safely.
- **Track rail anchor extraction:** Track anchors are unbolted from the concrete slab using heavy pneumatic and impact tools. Where anchors are chemically grouted or frozen, we flush-cut studs smoothly using abrasive discs without pitting the surrounding concrete.
- **Raised floor reinstatement:** In offices where compactor rails were recessed into raised access floors, we take extreme care not to buckle neighboring pedestal supports or disrupt underfloor electrical and data conduits.

Archive clearance pricing is calculated on a project-by-project basis depending on several operational and logistical variables:
