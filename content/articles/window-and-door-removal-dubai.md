## What affects the price of window and door disposal?

Because two lightweight bedroom doors and ten double-glazed villa patio sliders require completely different crew sizes and truck space, pricing is based on the physical job:

- **Quantity and dimensions:** Full-height sliding doors and wide picture windows require two-person or three-person carries and A-frame rack space.
- **Material mix:** Solid-core timber doors, heavy double-glazed units, and thick aluminium frames have different weights and sorting needs.
- **Glass condition:** Intact panels lift cleanly, while cracked or shattered panes need shatter-film taping and careful sweeping.
- **Floor and lift access:** Ground-floor villa driveways are quick to load, whereas high-rise towers require service elevator bookings and longer corridor carries.

## Preparing old windows and doors for collection

A quick check before our crew arrives keeps the pickup fast and safe:

- **Confirm full detachment:** Make sure all frames, sub-frames, and sills are completely unbolted from the concrete or blockwork.
- **Flag cracked glass early:** Tell us on WhatsApp if any double-glazed pane is broken so we bring extra shatter film and heavy-duty sweepers.
- **Keep walkways clear:** Move paint tins, scaffolding, and loose tools out of the hallway so our crew has a clear carry path.
- **Book the service lift:** For apartment buildings, reserve your renovation lift slot and share security contact details in advance.

## Free municipal pickup vs private renovation clearance

Dubai Municipality's free bulky-waste service does not collect home maintenance waste, renovation joinery, or glass-panelled building frames, and it does not serve freehold development zones or carry items down from high-rise apartments. For full details on municipal sorting and disposal rules, see our [construction waste disposal rules in Dubai](/construction-waste-disposal-rules-dubai/) guide. Using a licensed private crew keeps your renovation compliant and avoids community fines for kerbside dumping.
