## What affects the price of home cinema removal?

Every media room is built differently, so pricing reflects the actual labour, dismantling, and truck volume required:

- **Number and weight of seats:** Powered recliner rows weigh far more than standard sofas due to steel footrest mechanisms and motors.
- **Built-in risers:** Breaking down nailed or screwed timber platforms takes longer than lifting freestanding furniture.
- **Screen and wall stripping:** Fixed-frame screens up to 120–150 inches must be unclipped and dismantled indoors so they fit through doors.
- **Property access:** Basement staircases with tight landings or high-rise service elevator bookings affect carry-out time.

| Media room item | Dismantling method | Key preparation |
|---|---|---|
| Powered recliner rows | Unclip interlocking seat brackets | Unplug low-voltage power leads |
| Timber seating riser | Strip carpet, unscrew or cut frame | Disconnect any LED step-strip power |
| Fixed-frame / motorised screen | De-mount from wall, collapse frame | Isolate hardwired motor switch if fitted |
| AV rack and speakers | Disconnect loose cables, wheel out | Back up media servers or NAS drives first |

## Preparing your media room before collection

A few quick checks before our crew arrives will keep the strip-out fast and protect your remaining equipment:

- **Keep what you are reusing:** Remove personal Blu-rays, streaming boxes, gaming consoles, and any speakers or projectors you plan to keep.
- **Check hardwired switches:** Make sure ceiling projectors, motorised screens, or recessed step lights wired directly into wall spurs are isolated by an electrician.
- **Note concealed cables:** Let our crew know if you want in-wall HDMI or speaker cables left tucked inside the wall box rather than pulled out.
- **Book lift or gate access:** Share our trade licence and crew IDs with security or building management where move-out permits are required.
