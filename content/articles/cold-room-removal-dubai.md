When a restaurant lease ends, a food warehouse upgrades its storage, or a hotel kitchen undergoes a full strip-out, dismantling a walk-in chiller is rarely straightforward. Insulated wall panels are locked tightly together, heavy freezer doors sit on reinforced frames, and evaporator units are bolted high into the ceiling grid. Unlike standard shelving or loose catering appliances, a commercial cold room is a sealed structural enclosure built from dense polyurethane (PU) or polyisocyanurate (PIR) sandwich panels. Trying to force panels apart without the right tools damages surrounding kitchen tiles, cracks adjacent gypsum partitions, and leaves sharp aluminium extrusions exposed. Our experienced crew works step by step to strip, load, and clear the entire unit in one planned visit.

## What cold room components do we take?

Commercial refrigeration rooms vary from compact under-stairs beverage chillers to multi-bay warehouse freezer chambers. Our team dismantles and removes every non-hazardous structural and interior component associated with the unit:

- **PU and PIR insulated sandwich panels:** Wall and ceiling panels (75mm, 100mm, 150mm, and 200mm thicknesses) with galvanised steel or stainless steel skins.

## What affects the price of cold room removal?

Every cold room build is different, so pricing is based on practical on-site volume and dismantling complexity rather than guesswork. Sending photos on WhatsApp allows us to assess these exact factors before quoting a figure:

- **Cold room dimensions and height:** A small 2m × 2m restaurant chiller clears much faster than a 10m warehouse freezer chamber with 4-metre ceiling panels.
