## What affects the price of tyre disposal?

Because four sedan tyres in a villa garage and eighty truck tyres in an industrial yard require very different truck space and handling fees, we price each job transparently based on what is actually there:

- **Quantity and stack volume:** The total count of tyres determines truck space and loading time.
- **Tyre size and weight:** Heavy 4x4, truck, and solid forklift tyres take up far more volume and weight per unit than standard passenger tyres.
- **Rims attached vs loose tyres:** Tyres still mounted on steel or alloy rims require extra sorting and handling compared to bare rubber casings.
- **Access and staging:** Ground-floor workshop bays with direct truck backing are fastest, while basement parking levels or narrow residential alleys take a little more carrying.

## Preparing tyres for fast collection

A few quick steps make collection day effortless:

- **Drain standing water:** If tyres have been stored outdoors after rain or bay washing, tip out any trapped water so they are clean to load.
- **Separate hazardous fluids:** Keep used engine oil drums, brake fluid cans, and lead-acid batteries completely separate—we collect solid tyres and scrap hardware, not liquid automotive chemicals.
- **Confirm rim status:** Mention in your WhatsApp message whether any tyres are still on rims so our crew plans sorting accordingly.
- **Clear truck access:** Ensure service bays or villa driveways are clear for our truck to pull up close to the stack.

## Free municipal pickup vs private tyre removal

Dubai Municipality's free bulky-waste collection covers eligible household furniture and appliances only—it does not collect used vehicle tyres from residences or commercial garages. Trying to leave old tyres beside communal bins or inside general skips can trigger community fines or municipal violations. Booking a private licensed crew gives you direct pickup from your garage or workshop bay and ensures tyres are routed to approved rubber recycling channels.
