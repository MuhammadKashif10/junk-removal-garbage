## Handling high-density foam, plastics, and eco-friendly recycling

Childcare environments contain an unusually high concentration of synthetic materials that require responsible environmental diversion. Polyurethane foam from tumble mats and high-density plastics from playground sets can take hundreds of years to decompose if dumped into regional landfills.

Shanan Junk Removal is dedicated to sustainable, eco-friendly waste management across the United Arab Emirates:

- **HDPE and polypropylene plastics:** Large plastic playhouses, slides, and storage tubs are segregated and delivered to licensed plastics recycling plants in Al Quoz, where they are shredded and pelletized for manufacturing new consumer goods.
- **Timber and engineered wood:** Solid wood tables, chairs, and damaged MDF cubby shelves are directed to wood recycling processors for conversion into agricultural mulch, biomass, or industrial wood composite boards.
- **Scrap metal reclamation:** Steel cot frames, metal table legs, and aluminum whiteboard borders are separated and transported to licensed metal smelters for re-milling into structural rebar.
- **Polyurethane foam:** Clean foam sections are routed to commercial foam recyclers for shredding into carpet underlay padding wherever regional processing capacity allows.
