## What affects the price of generator removal?

Because a compact 5 kVA portable unit and a 100 kVA canopy diesel generator require completely different handling, we base pricing on the physical job details rather than guesswork:

- **Weight and dimensions:** Larger kVA ratings mean thicker steel skids, heavier engine blocks, and stricter payload requirements.
- **Access and rigging path:** Ground-floor loading bays with direct truck access are straightforward, whereas tight plant-room corridors, kerbs, or gravel yards take extra rigging time.
- **Plinth unbolting:** Units bolted into reinforced concrete with corroded anchor studs require extra cutting and floor make-good time.
- **Ancillary items:** Extra acoustic ducting, empty fuel cages, and steel exhaust pipes add to total load volume.

## Preparing your generator for safe pickup

A short checklist before collection day prevents delays and keeps everyone on site safe:

- **Confirm electrical isolation:** Ensure mains breaker feeds, earth straps, and control-panel cables are completely disconnected and capped by an electrician.
- **Drain all fluids:** Empty the fuel tank, sump oil, and cooling system completely so nothing leaks during tilting or transport.
- **Remove starter batteries:** Lead-acid batteries should be disconnected and handled through dedicated battery disposal streams.
- **Clear the exit route:** Move pallets, parked vans, or garden furniture out of the path between the generator pad and our truck bay.
