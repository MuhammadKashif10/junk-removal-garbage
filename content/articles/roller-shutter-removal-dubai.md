## What affects the price of roller shutter removal?

Shutter removals vary widely between a compact kiosk grille and a five-metre-tall industrial bay door, so pricing is based on practical site factors:

- **Width and height:** Wider, taller shutters have heavier steel axles and require taller step-towers or two-person controlled lowering.
- **Material and weight:** Thick galvanised steel warehouse curtains weigh significantly more than lightweight aluminium shopfront slats.
- **Motor and spring condition:** Jammed or crooked curtains trapped inside bent guide tracks require careful section-by-section cutting.
- **Work-hour restrictions:** Daytime industrial bay access is straightforward, whereas shopping malls require overnight de-fit scheduling.

## Preparing for roller shutter removal

Taking a few simple steps ahead of collection day keeps the dismantling fast and safe:

- **Isolate motor power:** Have an electrician disconnect any tubular motor, remote receiver, or key-switch from the mains circuit.
- **Clear the doorway zone:** Move stock pallets, display tables, or parked vehicles at least three metres back from the shutter opening.
- **Mention jammed or damaged tracks:** Let us know on WhatsApp if the shutter is stuck halfway or off its runners so our crew brings the right cutting and rigging gear.
- **Arrange permits or gate passes:** For malls, commercial towers, and gated communities, share our company documents with management a day ahead where required.
