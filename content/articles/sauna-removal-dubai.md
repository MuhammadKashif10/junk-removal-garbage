## What affects the price of sauna removal?

Because saunas range from simple clip-together infrared boxes to custom-built timber rooms, pricing is based on practical job details rather than a flat guess:

- **Size and capacity:** A single-person corner infrared cabin dismantles much faster than a four-person custom sauna with double-tier benches and large glass walls.
- **Construction style:** Prefabricated buckle-and-latch cabins come apart quickly, whereas custom-nailed timber framing glued or screwed into room walls takes longer to strip cleanly.
- **Glass and weight:** Heavy 8mm–10mm frameless tempered glass doors and large stove stone baskets require extra care and two-person carrying.
- **Access and building rules:** Ground-floor villa access is straightforward, while high-rise apartments require service elevator bookings and protective corridor padding.

## Preparing your home sauna before we arrive

Taking a few simple steps ahead of our arrival helps the dismantling go smoothly and keeps your handover schedule on track:

- **Clear personal items and towels:** Remove robes, essential oil bottles, wooden buckets, ladles, and loose headrests from inside the cabin.
- **Allow the heater and stones to cool:** Switch off the sauna at least 24 hours before collection so volcanic stones, ceramic infrared emitters, and timber benches are completely cool to handle.
- **Arrange electrical or plumbing isolation:** If the unit is hardwired into a wall spur or connected to a steam water line, have a qualified technician isolate and cap the connections beforehand.
- **Reserve the service lift or gate pass:** For apartments and gated villa communities, share our trade licence and crew IDs with security or building management a day in advance where required.
